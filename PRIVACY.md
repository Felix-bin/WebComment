# WebComment 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026-09-26

## 中文

WebComment（以下简称"本扩展"）是一个在网页上高亮文字和添加批注的浏览器扩展。

**我们不收集任何数据。**

- 你创建的高亮、批注，以及它们所在网页的地址和标题，只保存在你本机浏览器的扩展存储（`chrome.storage.local`）中。
- 本扩展不会把任何数据发送到开发者或任何第三方的服务器，不包含统计、广告或追踪代码，也不发起任何网络请求。
- 本扩展会读取你所访问网页的文字内容，仅用于在本地显示和恢复你的高亮，这些内容不会离开你的设备。
- 数据只有在你主动使用"导出备份 / 导出 Markdown / 复制"功能时才会以文件或剪贴板的形式交给你本人。
- 卸载本扩展时，浏览器会删除本扩展保存的全部数据。你也可以随时在"全部笔记"页面中删除数据。

如有疑问，请在 GitHub 提交 issue：https://github.com/Felix-bin/WebComment/issues

## English

WebComment is a browser extension for highlighting text and adding notes on web pages.

**We do not collect any data.**

- Your highlights, notes, and the URL and title of the pages they belong to are stored only in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
- The extension never sends data to the developer or any third party. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and makes no network requests.
- The extension reads the text of pages you visit solely to display and restore your highlights locally; that content never leaves your device.
- Data leaves the extension only when you explicitly use Export / Copy, and then only to you (as a file or via the clipboard).
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data. You can also delete data at any time from the "All notes" page.

Questions: https://github.com/Felix-bin/WebComment/issues
